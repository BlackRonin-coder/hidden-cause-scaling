# Scientific Context and Reading

These external references provide established background for the public research perspective. Their inclusion does **not** imply endorsement of this project or independent confirmation of its proposals.

## Indirect observation

- [NASA Science — Exoplanets](https://science.nasa.gov/exoplanets/): observing and characterising planets outside the Solar System.
- [Nobel Prize — Dorothy Crowfoot Hodgkin, Nobel Lecture (1964)](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1964/hodgkin/lecture/): historical context for structural inference through X-ray methods.
- [RCSB Protein Data Bank — Electron density maps](https://www.rcsb.org/docs/general-help/electron-density-maps-and-coefficient-files): modelling and interpreting structural evidence.

## Dark-matter evidence and searches

- [NASA Science — Dark Matter](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/dark-matter-dark-energy/dark-matter/): the observational motivation and open questions.
- [CERN — Dark Matter](https://home.cern/science/physics/dark-matter): collider and particle-physics context.
- [LUX-ZEPLIN — Dark Matter Search Results, 4.2 tonne-years (arXiv:2410.17036)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.17036): an example of experimental limits and how null findings constrain models.
- [ATLAS — Searching for Dark Matter](https://atlas.cern/updates/feature/dark-matter): indirect collider signatures and their interpretation.

## Reading this repository

These resources motivate the distinction between *measured effects*, *models that explain them* and *the hypotheses that remain open to testing*. They do not validate the example code or any unpublished hypotheses.
