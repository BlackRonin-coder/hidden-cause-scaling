# Discussion and Review

Constructive criticism is welcome, especially around:

- Whether the baseline model is adequate.
- Sources of noise, background, bias and alternative explanations.
- How to specify a useful out-of-sample prediction.
- What evidence would decisively falsify a proposed interpretation.
- Relevant published research or reproducibility practices.

Please distinguish **observations**, **interpretations** and **speculation**.

Do not submit private institutional data, unpublished personal records, sensitive credentials or confidential research assets. The public toy demonstration contains only synthetic data.

If proposing changes to the example, preserve its clearly illustrative nature and standard-library-only operation. Its test results do not establish an astrophysical discovery.
